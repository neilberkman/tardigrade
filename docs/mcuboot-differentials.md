Two profile configuration fixes were required so that the PR 2100 and PR 2109 pairs separate under `--quick --workers 2`:

1. PR 2100 `quick_use_heuristic: true`: Without `quick_use_heuristic: true`, `--quick` reduces the 15,557 calibrated writes to the 3 evenly spaced `quick_subset` points `0`, `7778`, and `15556`, missing the 3-write `fixup_revert` partial-magic window at write indices `3`, `4`, and `5` at `0x000F7FF4`, `0x000F7FF8`, and `0x000F7FFC` and causing `mcuboot_pr2100_broken.yaml` and `mcuboot_pr2100_broken_discovery.yaml` to exit 1 with 0 issue points. Setting `quick_use_heuristic: true` on all four PR 2100 profiles runs the 277 heuristic points under `--quick`, hitting the 3 `wrong_image` issue points on both broken profiles and 0 issue points on both fixed profiles.
2. PR 2109 `sweep_hash_bypass_symbols` removal: `mcuboot_pr2109_scratch_broken.yaml` and `mcuboot_pr2109_scratch_fixed.yaml` previously configured `sweep_hash_bypass_symbols: ["bootutil_img_validate"]`, which patched `bootutil_img_validate` at `0x00000504` to `movs r0, #0; bx lr` during faulted recovery runs. In broken commit `172694ea`, resuming an interrupted swap-scratch calls `boot_read_image_headers` at `0x00000a38` with non-NULL `&bs`, loading the 19 KB `slot0` header from `BOOT_SECONDARY_SLOT` into `state->imgs[0][0].hdr` while `BOOT_PRIMARY_SLOT` holds the 36 KB `slot1` image. `boot_validate_slot` at `0x000006cc` then calls `bootutil_img_validate` at `0x0000077a`, where `bootutil_tlv_iter_begin` at `0x00000568` reads offset `0x200 + 19,200` inside the 36 KB image, misses `IMAGE_TLV_INFO_MAGIC`, and returns `-1` so `context_boot_go` aborts into `FIH_PANIC`. Bypassing `bootutil_img_validate` masked that validation failure across all 312 heuristic points and all 6 quick points. Removing `sweep_hash_bypass_symbols` from `mcuboot_pr2109_scratch_broken.yaml` and `mcuboot_pr2109_scratch_fixed.yaml` restores real `bootutil_img_validate` execution, producing 3 `hard_fault` bricks at `w=11667`, `w=23334`, and `e=51` across the 6 quick points on `mcuboot_pr2109_scratch_broken.yaml` and 0 issues across the 6 quick points on `mcuboot_pr2109_scratch_fixed.yaml`.

# Removed Pairs

## PR 1597 Swap-Move g_last_idx Differential

The `mcuboot_pr1597_stm32f4_broken.yaml` and `mcuboot_pr1597_stm32f4_fixed.yaml` profiles, their STM32F429 ELF and commit assets, and `scripts/bootstrap_mcuboot_stm32f4_pr1597_assets.sh` are removed because broken commit `bfdf934e` recovers across all quick fault points on this 1-sector image geometry while fixed commit `2acc3b6a` faults on mid-swap recovery due to a cross-version `boot_read_swap_size` signature mismatch.

1. Calibration budget diagnosis on all three STM32F4 swap-move profiles: With the inherited nRF52 budgets `run_duration: "2.0"` and `max_step_limit: 20000000`, Phase 1 coarse calibration ran for 1.0 s of emulated time and stopped at `instruction_limit` of 100,000,000 instructions at 100 MIPS after 337,187 byte-program transitions and 5 erases on `mcuboot_pr1597_stm32f4_broken.yaml` and `mcuboot_pr1597_stm32f4_fixed.yaml`, and after 337,155 byte-program transitions and 6 erases on `mcuboot_stm32f4_upgrade.yaml`. Swapping one 128 KB, 131,072-byte STM32F4 Bank 1 sector at 1-byte write alignment requires `3 * 131,072 = 393,216` byte-program transitions across three sector copies plus trailer writes, totaling 393,242 `FLASH_CR.PG` transitions. At 100,000,000 instructions, 85.7 percent of the byte-program transitions had already completed and `exec` at `0x08020000` already matched `zephyr_stm32f4_slot1.bin`. Raising the budget to `run_duration: "3.3"` and `max_step_limit: 330000000` provides 50 percent headroom over the 2.2 s, 220,000,000-instruction completion point, where `mcuboot_pr1597_stm32f4_broken.yaml` and `mcuboot_pr1597_stm32f4_fixed.yaml` complete calibration at 48,028 changed-word writes and 5 erases with `vtor_captured` at `0x08022E3C`, and `mcuboot_stm32f4_upgrade.yaml` completes calibration at 48,050 changed-word writes and 6 erases and passes its `--quick` gate with exit code 0, control outcome `success`, 3 quick points `0`, `24025`, and `48049`, and 0 issue points.
2. Why broken `bfdf934e` recovers with 0 issues across all 3 quick points `0`, `24014`, and `48027`: The 16,216-byte staged image `zephyr_stm32f4_slot1.bin` fits inside a single 128 KB sector at `0x08020000`, so `last_idx = 1`. In `boot_read_image_header` at `0x08000c14`, `g_last_idx` at `0x20000008` is initialized to `0xFFFFFFFF` in `.data` on every reset and is only consulted in two branches: under `bs->op == BOOT_STATUS_OP_SWAP` guarded by `bs->idx > 1 && bs->idx <= g_last_idx` at `0x08000c58`, which is never entered on a 1-sector image because `bs->idx == 1`, and under `bs->op == BOOT_STATUS_OP_MOVE` for `slot == 0`, where `bs->idx <= g_last_idx` selects `off = 0` at `0x08020000` instead of `off = 0x20000` at `0x08040000`. Across writes `0..16,021` while primary sector 0 is copied to primary sector 1, no sector move status byte is written yet and `0x08020000` remains intact. Write `16,022` commits the single `BOOT_STATUS_OP_MOVE` status byte with `bs->op = 1` and `bs->idx = 2`, Erase 4 at write count `16,023` erases `0x08020000`, and writes `16,023..16,054` immediately write the 32-byte `image_header` of `zephyr_stm32f4_slot1.bin` to `0x08020000`. Across writes `16,054..32,023`, including quick midpoint `24,014`, reading `off = 0` returns a valid header yielding `g_last_idx = 1`, and `swap_run` completes the upgrade.
3. Why fixed `2acc3b6a` fails with `hard_fault` on mid-swap points `24014` and `48027`: `scripts/bootstrap_mcuboot_stm32f4_pr1597_assets.sh` transplanted `boot/bootutil/src/swap_move.c` from 2023 commit `2acc3b6a` into a detached worktree of MCUboot `HEAD` at `b893d34f`. In `2acc3b6a`, `boot_read_image_header` calls `boot_read_swap_size(0, &swap_size)` when `!boot_status_is_reset(bs)`, where the first parameter in 2023 was `int image_index = 0`. In MCUboot `HEAD`, `boot_read_swap_size(const struct flash_area *fap, uint32_t *swap_size)` takes a `const struct flash_area *` pointer, so integer literal `0` compiles as `fap = NULL` at `0x08000c2c: bl 0x8003d22 <boot_read_swap_size>` with `r0 = 0`. On the clean control boot and at `fault_at = 0`, `boot_status_is_reset(bs)` returns `1` and skips `boot_read_swap_size`. On mid-swap recovery boots at `fault_at = 24014` and `48027`, `boot_read_swap_size(NULL, &swap_size)` calls `boot_swap_info_off(NULL)` and `flash_area_read(NULL, ...)`, dereferencing aliased vector table words at `0x00000008..0x0000000c` and faulting into `arch_system_halt` at `0x08004D3C`.

## PR 2205 Scratch Geometry Differential

The `mcuboot_pr2205_scratch_broken.yaml` and `mcuboot_pr2205_scratch_fixed.yaml` profiles and their STM32F429 assets are removed because broken commit `c21c3909` and fixed commit `bbd0ee1e` both complete the upgrade on the control boot and recover on all 6 quick fault points across five attempted STM32F4 layouts:

1. Seeded pre-`cfded9d` single-bank layout with `slot0_partition` at `0x08008000` size `0x18000`, `slot1_partition` at `0x08020000` size `0x20000`, and profile `exec` and `staging` inverted: `update_trigger` wrote trailer magic to `BOOT_PRIMARY_SLOT`, returning `BOOT_SWAP_TYPE_NONE` with 0 writes and 0 erases, and unequal slot sizes fail `boot_slots_compatible`.
2. Dual-bank 128 KB layout with `slot0_partition` at `0x08020000` size `0x20000` across one 128 KB Bank 1 sector, `slot1_partition` at `0x08100000` size `0x20000` across four 16 KB and one 64 KB Bank 2 sectors, and `scratch_partition` at `0x08040000` size `0x20000` without `write-block-size = <8>`: 1-byte write alignment hit `stop_reason = instruction_limit` at 100,000,000 instructions after 300,374 writes and 7 erases.
3. Dual-bank 128 KB layout at `0x08020000` and `0x08100000` with `write-block-size = <8>` and the inherited nRF52 budgets `run_duration: "2.0"` and `max_step_limit: 20000000`: calibration terminated mid-swap at 23,425,504 instructions after 47,996 writes and 8 erases.
4. Dual-bank 128 KB layout at `0x08020000` and `0x08100000` with `write-block-size = <8>`, `run_duration: "20.0"`, and `max_step_limit: 100000000`: on a 1-sector primary slot, zero-initialized `sector_buffers.primary[0][1..4].fs_size` in `.bss` clamp broken `find_last_sector_idx = 4` back to `first_sector_idx = 0` and `sz = 0x20000` in `boot_copy_sz`, producing the exact same 1-sector swap plan of 22,739 writes and 8 erases as fixed.
5. Dual-bank 256 KB layout with `slot0_partition` at `0x08020000` size `0x40000` across two 128 KB Bank 1 sectors, `slot1_partition` at `0x08100000` size `0x40000` across four 16 KB, one 64 KB, and one 128 KB Bank 2 sectors, and `scratch_partition` at `0x08060000` size `0x20000` with `write-block-size = <8>`: on the 37,200-byte staged image, fixed `find_last_sector_idx` returns `0` and swaps 1 sector with 22,739 calibrated writes, 10 erases, and 49,164 control `FLASH_CR.PG` transitions, whereas broken `find_last_sector_idx` returns `4` and `boot_copy_sz` clamps through zero-initialized `.bss` entries to primary sector `1`, forcing `swap_run` to execute one extra swap iteration over the already-erased upper 128 KB half of both slots with 22,744 calibrated writes, 11 erases, and 97,151 control `FLASH_CR.PG` transitions.

That extra iteration over erased flash is not a resilience signal: unlike `bootsim`, where `primary_slot_sectors` lives on the stack and out-of-bounds indices read uninitialized stack bytes that trip an assertion, target builds place `sector_buffers` in `.bss` so out-of-bounds sector sizes read as `0`, and copying `0xFFFFFFFF` words across erased sectors preserves image contents, trailer state, and interrupted-swap recovery on control and all 6 quick fault points.

## PR 2206 Align-32 Geometry Differential

Both PR2206 profile pairs, `mcuboot_pr2206_scratch_broken.yaml`, `mcuboot_pr2206_scratch_fixed.yaml`, `mcuboot_pr2206_scratch_geom_broken.yaml`, and `mcuboot_pr2206_scratch_geom_fixed.yaml`, together with their dedicated ELFs and boundary images, are removed because the cycle-0 `wrong_image` verdict on `mcuboot_pr2206_scratch_geom_broken` does not come from swap-scratch trailer corruption.

Diffing MCUboot commits `e35461d2` and `08985c96` shows that `boot/bootutil/src/swap_scratch.c` only moves `get_first_trailer_sector` into `boot/bootutil/src/bootutil_misc.c` as `boot_get_first_trailer_sector`. The behavioral change in `08985c96` is in `bootutil_max_image_size` in `boot/bootutil/src/bootutil_misc.c`, which `bootutil_img_validate` calls before starting an upgrade. On the STM32F4 layout with `exec` at `0x08008000` size `0x18000` and `staging` at `0x08108000` size `0x18000` across two 16 KB sectors and one 64 KB sector, `scratch` at `0x08120000` size `0x20000`, `write_sz = 8`, `BOOT_MAX_ALIGN = 32`, and `BOOT_MAX_IMG_SECTORS = 2725`, `boot_trailer_sz` for 8-byte alignment equals `2725 * 24 + 160 = 0x10018`, placing the slot trailer start at `0x18000 - 0x10018 = 0x7FE8` so that `0x18` bytes spill into sector 1 at `0x4000..0x8000`. Broken `e35461d2` returns `boot_status_off` equal to `0x7FE8`, 32,744 bytes, from `bootutil_max_image_size`. Fixed `08985c96` subtracts `trailer_padding = scratch_trailer_sz - trailer_sz_in_first_sector = 0xB8 - 0x18 = 0xA0`, returning `0x7FE8 - 0xA0 = 0x7F48`, 32,584 bytes.

When staging holds the 32,720-byte, `0x7FD0`, boundary image `zephyr_slot1_scratch_geom_pr2206_boundary.bin`:

1. Broken `e35461d2` evaluates `0x7FD0 <= 0x7FE8` in `bootutil_img_validate`, accepts the staged image, and swaps it into `exec` across `12,295` calibrated changed-word writes and `8` page erases with `12,297` control `FLASH_CR.PG` transitions, booting `exec` at `vtor = 0x08008200` and `pc = 0x0800AE54` with UART output `"Hello World! nucleo_f429zi/stm32f429xx"`. Because `scratch_partition` at `0x08120000` occupies a 128 KB, `0x20000`, physical sector, `scratch_trailer_off = 0x20000 - 0xB8 = 0x1FF48` stays well above `copy_sz = 0x3FE8` while swapping sector 1 at `0x4000..0x8000`, and every byte of `zephyr_slot1_scratch_geom_pr2206_boundary.bin` across `0x0000..0x7FD0` is copied intact into `exec`.
